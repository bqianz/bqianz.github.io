Plein-air painting is the act of painting outdoors. The theme of this year's [Pleinairpril](https://www.warriorpainters.com/pleinairpril) challenge is Harmony in Contrast. My goal of doing this challenge is to improve my skills in picking and matching colours. 

The concept of plein air painting is peak “romanticizing everyday life” but reality is cold…April in Vancouver is still quite chilly. So some of these paintings are based on what I see in real life and some of these are based on photos I find online.

#### Day 07: Blending Mode
- En Plein Air: No
- Time spent: 2.5 hours
- Reference: [1]({{ page.assetPath }}/ref07.jpg)
- This is probably my favourite in this challenge. I first brushed in big colour blocks, then went on to render half of the picture, at which point I became very tired of rendering. So I wanted to do a different style for the other half of the picture. I played around with layer blending modes and arrived at something that made the colours look retro-cartoony and cool (I can't remember exactly but the blending mode might have been "difference"). Then I finished the rendering on the cartoony half with some stylized brushes.

- Lost my steam and stopped the challenge here.

My retrospective thought is that colour is so closely related to composition, which to me is the rhythm of blocks of colours based on value and saturation, and a little bit on hue. When I was drawing from pictures the composition was already given and already beautiful, so I wasn't really practicing the thought process on composition (which is the most difficult thing for me when I'm drawing original pieces). Next year if I do this again I will probably switch things up so that I am forced to think hard about composition.
