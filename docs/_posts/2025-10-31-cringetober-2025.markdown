### Day 18: Alternate Universe
![18](/assets/2025-10-31-cringetober-2025/18_au.jpg)
Boy band AU of three male characters from [Road to Empress](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3478050/Road_to_Empress_I/). This was fun deciding on the the colour scheme and styling of each character, with [Li Zhi](https://road-to-empress.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Zhi) in the front with his usual crisp and clean style, [Li Tai](https://road-to-empress.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Tai) with his signature navy blue and slut strands, and [Bian Ji](https://road-to-empress.fandom.com/wiki/Bianji) with his lack of hair and buddhist-monk-inspired robe.

## Epilogue
Drawing with fun prompts like these brought me back to how I enjoyed drawing as a kid. I felt **fulfilled** for the first time in decades(?) doing this challenge, it made me think, maybe my life's purpose is to create beautiful things and convey fun ideas.
