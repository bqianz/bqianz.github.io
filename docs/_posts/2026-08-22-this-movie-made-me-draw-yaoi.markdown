### Good things in this drawing
- the muscles look less unnaturally plump compared to my last drawing
- elongation of the chest and shoulder muslces is observable
- probably the right decision to go black and white because originally I did it in colour and it looked like a mess
- tried to add some background, not sure how I feel about the quality of the bg but it does look better than before I added the background

### Bad things in this drawing
- used a watercolor brush to fill in areas, the shading looks unorganized
- took too long to complete

### Things to remember for next time
- if the goal is to not take too long with a piece, try neater lines and more simple shanding (three values at most)
