### Difficult Process
This was a free commission requested by the Weibo Fanclub of the ship between the female lead and second male love interest of the game [Road to Empress](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3478050/Road_to_Empress_I/).

This was my first experience with commissions. Fortunately I had complete freedom over the content of the drawing as long as it involves these two characters, but it was still unexpectedly difficult for me because of the external deadline and my lack of ideas. (Lowkey also from the thought at the back of my head that my work will be compared to those from other artists involved in this fan event.)

### Fan Event
Everything turned out alright and I managed to submit it on time. The event itself spanned three days, where a fan-produced work was released in the topic forum every hour. It was really fun seeing other people's artworks, stories, and edits, and seeing the fans' reactions. 

### Trading Cards
A few weeks later the fanclub organizers asked me if they can use my drawing as a trading card in the virtual gacha within the topic forum. Of course I said yes, I would love for more people to see my drawing. 

So below is a collection of trading cards made from fanarts of this ship. It feels very rewarding, in a collective way if that makes sense, to see the hard work and vision of so many artists gathered in one place like this. Like I am part of something great.
