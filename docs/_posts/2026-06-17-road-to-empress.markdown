Fanart of the interactive film game [Road to Empress](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3478050/Road_to_Empress_I/).

### The game
Road to Empress Part 1 was a great game; it was entertaining and just the right length. Ridiculous things were happening to the protagonist and I really felt like my choices in the game mattered in where the plot is heading.

Unfortunately, Road to Empress Part 2 got rid of the entertaining part of the game which is the role-playing aspect (due to perhaps external pressure to stick to [true history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu_Zetian)); what remained was a melodramatic interpretation of the Empress' rise to the throne and her (mostly fictional) love triangle with the two male leads. Part II did not feel like I was playing a game, it felt like I was watching a mediocore Chinese drama.

### This drawing got clout
This drawing got a lot of likes on Chinese online platforms, and I think it's not because my show of art skills was spectacular here, but because so many people went into Part II with high expectations and felt let down by the plot points, especially between the protagonist and second male love interest. The fans yearn to see these two fighting side by side; I believe this was the idea in this drawing that resonated so widely.
